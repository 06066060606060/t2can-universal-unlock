TMR PORT MANIFEST
=================
Board: ESP32-S3
CAN controllers: 3 x MCP2515 / XL2515
CAN bitrate: 500 kbit/s
MCP2515 crystal: 16 MHz
Shared SPI: SCK GPIO12, MOSI GPIO11, MISO GPIO13
J2 BODY: CS GPIO10, STBY GPIO18
J3 CHASSIS: CS GPIO9, STBY GPIO14
J4 PARTY: CS GPIO8, STBY GPIO21
GPIO9 is exclusively J3 CHASSIS CS and is never reset.
All profiles: VEHICLE_TOPOLOGY_PARTY_BODY_CHASSIS. VH is an alias of BODY.
